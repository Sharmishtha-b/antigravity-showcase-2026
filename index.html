Executive Insights & Strategic Recommendations

  • Space is the Ultimate Premium: Floor area alone explains nearly half (46.6%) of the variance in HDB resale prices. Every additional square meter translates significantly to the final transaction caveat.
  • Location Hierarchy: Town location is the second most crucial driver (21.3% variance). Premium mature estates like Central Area and Bukit Timah command over $6,400 to $7,400 per square meter, nearly double the unit rate of non-mature estates like Choa Chu Kang and Woodlands (~$3,500/sqm).
  • Lease Decay Matters: Remaining lease explains ~17.7% of price variance. Buyers actively discount older flats, emphasizing the importance of asset lifespan in long-term affordability planning.
  • Strategic Recommendation for Buyers: First-time homebuyers prioritizing space over location should target non-mature estates in the North and West regions to maximize square footage per dollar. Conversely, those prioritizing long-term capital preservation should balance mature locations with healthy remaining lease durations (>60 years).
